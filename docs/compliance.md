# TenderHub Compliance

## 1. Overview

TenderHub manages vendor compliance requirements and supporting documentation throughout the procurement lifecycle.

The compliance workflow is:

```text
Compliance Requirement
        ↓
Vendor Compliance Record
        ↓
Supporting Document
        ↓
Review
        ↓
Compliant / Non-Compliant
        ↓
Expiry Monitoring
        ↓
Renewal / Re-Verification
