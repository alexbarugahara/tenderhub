# TenderHub Vendor Workflow

## 1. Overview

TenderHub provides vendors with a structured workflow for discovering procurement opportunities, maintaining company information, preparing bids, submitting responses, and managing awards and contracts.

The core vendor workflow is:

```text
Vendor Registration
      ↓
Vendor Profile
      ↓
Documents / Certifications / Compliance
      ↓
Solicitation Discovery
      ↓
Solicitation Review
      ↓
Save Solicitation
      ↓
Create Bid
      ↓
Complete Requirements
      ↓
Upload Bid Documents
      ↓
Validate Bid
      ↓
Submit Bid
      ↓
Bid Locked
      ↓
Review / Evaluation
      ↓
Award
      ↓
Contract
