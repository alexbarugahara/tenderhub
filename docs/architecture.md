# TenderHub Architecture

## 1. Overview

TenderHub is a modular procurement management platform built to support the complete procurement lifecycle between organizations and vendors.

The current architecture is centered around the following domain:

```text
Organization
     ↓
Procurement
     ↓
Solicitation
     ↓
Lot
     ↓
Requirements
     ↓
Vendor
     ↓
Bid
     ↓
Evaluation
     ↓
Award
     ↓
Contract
